# SignalScope — SIH 2026 Required Model Report
### *Telling Real From Synthetic in the Age of Generative Media*

| FIELD | SIGNALSCOPE SPECIFICATION & RESULTS |
| :--- | :--- |
| **TASK** | **Binary Image Authenticity Classification (`likely_real` vs. `likely_ai_generated`) with Responsible Uncertainty (`uncertain`).**<br>SignalScope detects synthetic AI imagery across modern generator families (Diffusion, GANs, Flow Matching) while preventing false accusations through calibrated likelihood and perturbation stability. **Implemented SIH Bonus Modules:** (A) Faithful Explanation (Grad-CAM & 2D FFT), (C) Robustness to Degradation (Authenticity Stability Score $S$), (D) Provenance & Metadata (EXIF & C2PA), and (F) Real-Time / Deployable Interface (FastAPI on Cloud Run + Next.js workstation). *(Modules B, E, and G full active defense are omitted to prevent overconfident claims).* |
| **DATA & SPLIT** | **Official SIH Data:** 100,000 images (50,000 REAL, 50,000 FAKE, 32×32 JPEG) split deterministically into **85,000 train / 15,000 validation** (seed 42, zero overlap).<br>**External Data (version-2):** Disclosed public benchmark `AIGenImages2026` containing **9,758 train** and **1,118 validation** samples across 19 modern generator families. *Crucial audit finding: AIGenImages2026 contains strictly paired `0_real` and `1_fake` classes.* Additional 5,004 photographic real images from `REAL/` added to balance multi-resolution training.<br>**Untouched Organizer Test Partition:** In strict adherence to competition rules, the organizer-provided SIH held-out test partition (`SignalScope-data/test`) was **kept completely untouched** (0 accesses) and was not used for training, validation, tuning, or reported scoring. |
| **MODEL / APPROACH** | **Backbone:** ConvNeXt-Tiny (`convnext_tiny.in12k_ft_in1k`, ~28.6M parameters) initialized with ImageNet-12k pretraining and fine-tuned for forensic artifact detection.<br>**Training Hyperparameters:** AdamW optimizer, cosine annealing schedule, base learning rate $3 \times 10^{-5}$, weight decay $1 \times 10^{-2}$, batch size 64, mixed precision (FP16), 5 epochs with early stopping (best: epoch 3).<br>**Forensic Augmentations:** Multi-scale RandomResizedCrop (0.8–1.0), simulated JPEG recompression ($p=0.30$, quality 65–95), ColorJitter (0.1), mild Gaussian blur ($p=0.20$, $\sigma \in [0.1, 1.0]$), and horizontal flips.<br>**Calibration:** Post-hoc Temperature Scaling ($T = 0.9986$) fitted via L-BFGS on a 2,000-sample disjoint split to eliminate overconfident logit extremes.<br>**Supporting Evidence Stack:** (1) Grad-CAM spatial attribution on Stage 3 Block 2; (2) Centered 2D FFT log-magnitude spectrum & High-Frequency Energy Ratio; (3) Authenticity Stability Score $S \in [0, 1]$ across 4 controlled web degradations; (4) EXIF tags & C2PA credentials inspection. |
| **METRIC & RESULT** | **Primary External Cross-Generator Validation ($N = 1,118$ across 19 generator families, `AIGenImages2026/val`):**<br>• **ROC-AUC:** **0.9220** (Generator Macro-Average AUC: **0.9237**)<br>• **Macro-F1:** **0.8578** (85.78%)<br>• **Selected Operating Threshold:** **0.6338**<br>• **Accuracy at Selected Threshold:** **86.40%** (Default 0.50 threshold: 85.78%)<br>• **False Positive Rate (FPR):** **9.84%** at selected threshold (Default 0.50 threshold: 13.95%)<br>• **Calibration Quality:** Expected Calibration Error (ECE) = **0.0664**; Brier Score = **0.1098**<br>**Local In-Domain Validation ($N = 15,000$, official SIH split):** ROC-AUC: **0.9991** \| Macro-F1: **0.9851** \| Accuracy: **98.51%** \| FPR: **1.85%** \| ECE: **0.0086** \| Brier: **0.0119**.<br>**Verified Confusion Matrices:**<br>• *External Cross-Generator Validation ($N = 1,118$, threshold = 0.6338):* **TN = 504 \| FP = 55 \| FN = 97 \| TP = 462** *(Real Acc: 90.2%, Synthetic Recall: 82.6%)*<br>• *Local Baseline In-Domain Validation ($N = 15,000$, threshold = 0.50):* **TN = 7,423 \| FP = 77 \| FN = 61 \| TP = 7,439**<br>*(Notice: External cross-generator validation AUC = 0.9220. Official SIH held-out overall and unseen-generator AUC are reserved for organizer evaluation).* |
| **BASELINE** | **Baseline Model Comparison (`signalscope-baseline-v1` vs. `signalscope-v2`):**<br>The original baseline (trained exclusively on 32×32 SIH images) achieved near-perfect in-domain validation (ROC-AUC: **0.9992**, Macro-F1: **0.9908**), but suffered catastrophic domain collapse when evaluated on modern high-resolution photography and unseen generators: external cross-generator AUC fell to **0.5933**, flagging **89.09% of real high-resolution photographs as synthetic** due to high-frequency edge sensitivity.<br>**Independent Photographic-Real Holdout Gate ($N = 9,000$ unused authentic photos across 9 categories):**<br>• Baseline v1 False Positive Rate: **74.69%** (6,722 false alarms)<br>• Production v2 False Positive Rate: **1.37%** (123 false alarms) $\rightarrow$ **98.17% relative false-alarm reduction** (Specificity: **98.63%**).<br>• Median synthetic probability on real photos dropped from **0.9956** down to **0.0019**.<br>**Conclusion:** `signalscope-v2` was selected to eliminate the baseline's real-world generalization failure while preserving 0.9991 in-domain discrimination. |
| **LIMITATIONS** | 1. **Organizer Held-Out Test Status:** Official unseen-generator score is pending organizer evaluation as `test/` was kept strictly untouched.<br>2. **Generator Asymmetry:** Performance varies across generative families; weakest observed candidate generator is `midjourney_v7` (AUC: **0.7582**, recall: 67.7% @ 0.50), reflecting modern ultra-smooth texturing.<br>3. **Degradation Brittleness on Borderline Cases:** Severe resolution decimation (0.75× downsampling) and screenshot simulation induce probability drift ($\Delta p \approx 0.13$), safely caught by the Authenticity Stability gate ($S < 0.60 \rightarrow$ Uncertain).<br>4. **Attribution Is Non-Causal:** Grad-CAM saliency highlights influential convolutional feature regions; it does not constitute pixel-level legal proof of tampering.<br>5. **Metadata Stripping Neutrality:** Absence of EXIF/C2PA metadata is non-informative due to social platform re-encoding.<br>6. **Scope Boundary:** Not designed for biometric identity verification, deepfake facial profiling, or real-world political adjudication. |
