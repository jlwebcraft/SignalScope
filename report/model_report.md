# SignalScope: Model & Evaluation Report
**"Telling Real From Synthetic in the Age of Generative Media"**  
*SIH 2026 Internal Hackathon*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Scope

SignalScope addresses the challenge of distinguishing authentic camera captures from synthetic media generated by deep generative models (GANs, Latent Diffusion Models, Autoregressive visual models).

### Ethical Guardrails
- **No Identity Profiling**: The system does not identify, profile, or track individual people.
- **No Absolute Accusations**: Outputs are framed probabilistically (`Likely AI-generated`, `Likely real`, `Uncertain`).
- **Conflict & Degradation Transparency**: Surfacing disagreement between modality branches and degradation volatility rather than projecting artificial confidence.

## 2. Methodology & Architecture

### Dual-Branch Multimodal Evidence Fusion
```
                   +------------------------+
                   |      Input Image       |
                   +-----------+------------+
                               |
              +----------------+----------------+
              |                                 |
              v                                 v
   +--------------------+             +--------------------+
   | RGB Spatial Branch |             |  Frequency Branch  |
   |   ConvNeXt-Tiny    |             |   2D FFT/DCT Spec  |
   +----------+---------+             +---------+----------+
              |                                 |
              +----------------+----------------+
                               |
                               v
                     +-------------------+
                     |  Evidence Fusion  |
                     +---------+---------+
                               |
                               v
                    +--------------------+
                    |    Calibration     |
                    +----+----------+----+
                         |          |
            +------------+          +------------+
            v                                    v
   +-----------------+                  +-----------------+
   | Authenticity    |                  | Grounded        |
   | Verdict         |                  | Explanation     |
   +-----------------+                  +-----------------+
```

### Authenticity Stability Score
Images are subjected to controlled transformations:
1. **Pristine Original**
2. **JPEG Recompression** (Quality = 70)
3. **Resolution Rescaling** (Downsample 0.6x then Bicubic Upsample)
4. **Screenshot Simulation** (Subpixel blur + re-encoding)
5. **Light Crop & Resize** (92% frame crop)

Predictions across transformations are evaluated for variance and maximum drift.

## 7. Known Limitations & Failure Cases
1. **Extreme Compression**: Aggressive multi-pass social media recompression (e.g. WhatsApp / Discord low-bitrate compression) dampens high-frequency generative artifacts.
2. **Hybrid / Edited Media**: Images combining authentic camera backgrounds with synthetic in-painting require patch-level localization.
3. **Novel Architectures**: Unseen generative paradigms (e.g., discrete diffusion or novel autoregressive tokenizers) may require ongoing domain-adaptation fine-tuning.
